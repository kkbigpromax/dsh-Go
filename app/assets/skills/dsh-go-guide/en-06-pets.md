# Streamlined edition

This edition keeps native chat, the local engine, custom providers and subscription sign-in, plugins, speech and Phone control. Remote hosts (SSH and DSH Remote), the DeepSeek account entry, DS Bot, Pets, Companion Room, Tavern, the original DSH web interface, the left-swipe shortcut panel, and the Help and About settings group and related controls are removed. DeepSeek models can be added as a custom provider.

Chat completion, failure and approval notifications remain available in notification settings.
